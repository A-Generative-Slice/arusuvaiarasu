## Key Psychological Triggers Used
1. **Respect for their Legacy**: Mentions Dr. Nataraja Iyer, V.V. Giri, Kumbakonam Elai Saapaadu, and their 60k Instagram patrons.
2. **Speed as the Differentiator**: Solves the real friction in Chennai wedding catering: *speed to quotation*. The first caterer to send a royal, itemized PDF quote usually locks the 25% advance.
3. **Hyper-Local Closeness**: Emphasizes that "A Generative Slice" is on Valluvar Kottam High Rd (Nungambakkam / Kodambakkam border), just 10 minutes from West Mambalam. Traditional business owners prefer local partners they can meet face-to-face.
