# Commercial Packaging & Pricing Strategy for Arusuvai Arasu

## Executive Strategy
Arusuvai Arasu is not a small startup catering business. They have catered **75,000+ weddings**, served **1,00,000 guests in a single day**, and handled VIP clients like the **Ambani family** and **Padma Shri Dr. K.J. Yesudas**. 

When presenting commercials to Mr. Sridhar, anchor against the scale of a single wedding:
* Average wedding catering revenue for Arusuvai Arasu (800 - 1500 guests, 3-4 sessions): **₹8,00,000 to ₹25,00,000**.
* Profit margin per wedding: **20% to 30%** (~₹2.5L - ₹7.5L).
* Therefore, winning or accelerating **just ONE wedding booking** that would have otherwise gone to a competitor (due to quoting delay) pays for this software multiple times over.

## ROI Pitch Talking Points for In-Person Meeting
1. **Response Time = Advance Paid**: In Chennai, high-budget families inquire with 2 or 3 top-tier caterers (e.g., Arusuvai Arasu, Sree Ambaal, Gnanambika). The caterer who provides an itemized, royal PDF quotation within 5 minutes wins the confidence of the family elders.
2. **Instagram Monetization**: You have 60,000+ followers on Instagram watching your mouth-watering *Elai Saapaadu* reels. Right now, only a tiny fraction DM you. With this tool linked in your bio, parents and NRI brides in the US/UK/Singapore can build their dream menu and request a formal quote in 60 seconds.
3. **Local Partner Commitment**: "A Generative Slice is located on Valluvar Kottam High Rd. We are your neighbors. If your staff needs training or you need menu updates before the next wedding season, our team is right around the corner."
