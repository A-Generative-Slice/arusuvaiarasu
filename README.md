## About the Application
A bespoke, high-conversion banquet quoting engine and multi-hall catering operations manager designed specifically for **Arusuvai Arasu Caterers**, honoring the culinary legacy of **Dr. Arusuvai Nataraja Iyer** (entitled by former Indian President Dr. V.V. Giri, with 75,000+ weddings served since 1952).

### Key Modules:
1. **⚡ Instant 60-Second PDF Banquet Quote Generator**:
   - Multi-session Muhurtham builder (Viratham, Janavasam High Tea, Muhurtham Breakfast, Grand Kalyana Virundhu Elai Saapaadu, Grand Evening Reception).
   - Dynamic Pax calculations across 3 culinary tiers (*Classic Heritage*, *Royal Arusuvai*, *Presidential Maharaja*).
   - Live costing breakdown with 5% catering GST and 25% date-booking advance.
   - 1-Click Client-side PDF generator (`html2pdf.js`) compiling an authorized letterhead proposal in seconds.
   - Instant WhatsApp quote sharing hook.

2. **🏛️ Chennai Multi-Hall Inventory & Date Slot Tracker**:
   - Real-time availability tracking across 6 premier Chennai wedding venues:
     - Radisson Blu Resort & Temple Bay (Mahabalipuram)
     - Mayor Ramanathan Chettiar Hall (MRC Hall, Santhome)
     - Sri Vari Kalyana Mandapam (Vanagaram)
     - Raja Muthiah & Rani Meyyammai Mandapam (Egmore)
     - Em El Em Kalyana Mandapam (Mahalingapuram)
     - AVM D Rajeshwari Kalyana Mandapam (Mylapore)
   - Muhurtham season date filtering (Nov 2026, Dec 2026, Jan 2027, Feb 2027).
   - Status indicators: **Available**, **Tentative Inquiry**, and **Advance Locked**.
   - Live dispatch tracker for Master Chef teams, traditional heavy brass urn sets, and Agmark raw materials.

3. **👑 VIP Patrons & Heritage Showcase**:
